You are told to take 5 feeders from any department; the idea is that the feeders for this puzzle are all drinks or food containing some combinations of fruits and alcohol. The feeders and corresponding fruits + alcohol are:

BANANAS FOSTER	-> BANANA + RUM  
CASINO		-> CHERRY + ORANGE + LEMON + GIN  
GIMLET		-> LIME + GIN  
MARGARITA		-> LIME + ORANGE + TEQUILA  
PARADISE		-> ORANGE + APRICOT + GIN

The circles along the top row represent the alcohol and the circles along the bottom row represent the fruit. The color and the combinations of arrows clue what the placement of feeders into the middle row is, and what the alcohol and fruit to consider are.
