So, in this race, Toynbee wins with 5 votes, and Pretty wins with 3\.

However, after we submit these two winners, it turns out there’s a recount\! Each of the judges have a different idea of how to count the votes. In particular, there’s disagreement about how to handle the **chads**; the chads present in this puzzle are _hanging_, _swinging_, and _tri chads_.
