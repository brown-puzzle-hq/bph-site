After identifying each of the characters, solvers may notice that the character names are one letter away from bird names:

| Character | Bird   | New Letter | Old Letter |
| --------- | ------ | ---------- | ---------- |
| Pinch     | Finch  | F          | P          |
| Hoople    | Hoopoe | O          | L          |
| Aillet    | Willet | W          | A          |
| Yark      | Lark   | L          | Y          |

Reading the new letters and then old letters in order, solvers obtain the answer **FOWL PLAY**.
