First, we solve the clues:
1. Famous football family: Manning

3. Fugi-\_\_\_\_\_, the avian criminal: Dove
4. Star Wars: A New \_\_\_\_\_: Hope

All these are streets in Providence. Locating these streets on the given map, one sees the names have been altered. (Hence the name of the puzzle [Trap streets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trap_street)). Each of these streets has had a letter dropped (or given away). Ordering dropped letters by question order obtains the solution.

1. Manning → Mannin: G
1. Williams → Willams: I
1. Dove → Doe: V
1. Hope → Hop: E
1. Villa → Vill: A
1. Brown → Bron: W
1. Amy → My: A
1. Cady → Cad: Y

The solution is **GIVE AWAY**.
