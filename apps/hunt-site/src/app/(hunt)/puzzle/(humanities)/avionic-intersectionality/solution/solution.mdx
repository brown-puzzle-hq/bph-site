This puzzle is a kriss-kross. It works like a crossword, but the clues aren’t numbered. They are separated into across and down in alphabetical order of their answers. The solvers should answer all of them and then figure out where they all fit into the grid.

The first twist is that there are rebuses in this puzzle. Every square that is greyed is a rebus. Furthermore, the rebuses are all airport codes of US capitals. This is hopefully clued by the title. 

There are also dots on the grid. Each of these dots is actually a US state capital (with the start as DC). They should overlay a map of the US on the grid using the dots as lines. Then, look for words with two rebuses. These are flights. They should draw out all the flights (ie. GLOB<u>ALB</u>E<u>ATL</u>ESDAY goes from Albany to Atlanta). Each flight will cross another flight both on the map and on the grid (so GLOB<u>ALB</u>E<u>ATL</u>ESDAY crosses with AME<u>RIC</u>AN<u>BIS</u>ON and the Albany to Atlanta flight crosses with Richmond to Bismarck).
