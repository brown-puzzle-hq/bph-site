Solvers are presented with cryptic clues with no definitions, but may be clued from the flavortext that the answers are all Clash Royale cards.

Solving these clues and indexing into their names by their elixir cost results in the following (indicators are italicized, fodder is underlined):

| Clue | Answer | Elixir cost | Indexed letter |
| ---- | ------ | ----------- | -------------- |
| <u>Knight which is</u> *hard of hearing* | NIGHT WITCH | 4 | H |
| <u>Braveheart actor</u> *is confused without* <u>myself</u> | GOBLINS | 2 | O |
| *Hiding* <u>activates lasers</u> | TESLA | 4 | L |
| *Crazed* <u>admirer</u> *starts* <u>relapsing</u> | RAM RIDER | 5 | I |
| *Frustrated* <u>Strip Weathers</u> *not* <u>electric</u> *after* <u>dongle</u> *accident* | GOLDEN KNIGHT | 4 | D |
| <u>Lawyer test</u> *twice* and <u>(Magic Johnson rapper and Hecox of Smosh)</u> | BARBARIANS | 5 | A |
| <u>Jimin’s family</u> *surrounded by* <u>Gangnam Style singer’s</u> *sound* | SPARKY | 6 | Y |

Reading the indexed letters in order gives HOLIDAY. The flavortext is asking for a new 1-elixir card that fits the enumeration (7 6). Nearly all 1-elixir cards are spirits, so the answer is **HOLIDAY SPIRIT**.
