Going clockwise (starting from 0) and taking the first letter gives the cluephrase INDEX MODULO WORD LENGTH INCREASING ORDER.

Indexing into the words in increasing order (1 to 36) using numbers modulo the word's length gives the final cluephrase of NEW YORK NEW YORK OR MONTE CARLO SIX LETTERS.

New York-New York and Monte Carlo are both examples of a **CASINO**, which is the answer to the puzzle.
